Eco Badge Tiers

Our sustainability commitment is not one-size-fits-all. We offer multiple Eco Tags based on a business’s contribution:

EcoStarter™

Businesses supporting global reforestation through their Pepagora plan.

EcoSeller™

Businesses offering verifiably eco-friendly or recycled products.

EcoVerified™

Businesses holding official green certifications (e.g., FSC, GOTS, ISO 14001).

EcoChampion™

Companies with audited, significant commitments to sustainable operations..

 Note: All badges are prominently displayed on profiles and are filterable in search, helping buyers find and choose trusted partners faster.
