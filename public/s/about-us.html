A 25-Year Mission to Understand One Problem

Our story doesn't begin in a boardroom. It begins in the dusty industrial towns and crowded workshops where real business happens.

For over two decades, our founder, Murugesan Dhanapal, wasn't just selling to SMEs he was studying them. He sat with them, listened, and learned.

I saw businesses producing world-class products, yet they were invisible online. They were misunderstood by tech, underserved by platforms, and overlooked by capital. The gap was clear: existing platforms gave them a listing. They needed a launchpad.”

I've spent over 3 hours each with more than 10,000 businesses—not to sell, but to understand. I heard their struggles, saw their potential, and witnessed their frustration.

Why "Pepagora"?

It comes from two roots:

“pep” - energy, momentum, spark

“agora” - the ancient Greek word for marketplace

Put together, Pepagora means: "A vibrant marketplace powered by action."
